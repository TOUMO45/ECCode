Key fact: 7 of the 13 are in the verifier corpus (correct.json c-*-nat*), also present in tune (7/429). Only 6 are holdout (hold-03,07,11, hold-inj-04,05,06).
Categories (13):
A. Sentence-initial capitalised ordinary word treated as name by rule (iv) capitalisation: Roughly x3 (nat5,nat7,nat17), Half (nat20), Running (hold-11), Billing (nat13, weakest: subject noun not in source, arguably a defensible flag) = 6

C. WEAK_SUPPORT (stem = canon+5-char truncation, no -ed/-ing handling; generic reporting verbs counted as content): nat3 (decided~Decision), nat4 (completed~finished, falling), hold-03 (worked vs works), hold-inj-05 (finished/building vs built) = 4
D. known-name set polluted by long adversarial author string (addName splits whole author text; known check precedes stoplist so "the" counts as a name): hold-inj-06 = 1
Prototype general rules (scratchpad p/), tested on corpus+tune only:
 1. rule (iv): sentence-initial capitalised token exempt only if it ends -ly/-ing (NOT blanket exemption: that broke M1b f-13 Priya, f-18 Fatima).
 2. stoplist adds: half halves quarter third twice double roughly approximately nearly; decided completed finished confirmed reported (none in forbidden-stopwords.txt).
 3. stem(): strip -ing/-ed before 5-char truncation.
 4. buildContext: addName only for author strings of <=3 words (whole handle still known).
Result: M1b 63/63, probes flagged 2/6 (unchanged), M1c corpus+tune 0/429 (was 7/429). Not run on holdout.
Not developable from tune/corpus alone: demonym rule (B) and rule 4 (D); no tune/corpus case exhibits them. Sketch for B: purely alphabetic token >=6 chars with suffix -ian/-an/-ish/-ese/-ean supported by a source token sharing a >=5 char prefix (must not apply to hyphenated/digit identifiers or f-style payments-api vs payments-gateway). Needs new synthetic tune cases written from the principle, plus fabrication twins, before adoption.
