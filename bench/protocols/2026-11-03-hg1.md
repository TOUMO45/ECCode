# Pre-registered protocol: H-G1, generalization of the frozen detector v3 to a second FastAPI codebase

Committed **before any candidate codebase is browsed**; the git commit timestamp is the registration. Where this protocol says nothing, `2026-11-03-hb3.md` (and through it `2026-10-28-phase2.md`) applies. Standing rules unchanged: read before write, pre-register before measuring, freeze before seeds, labels before figures, evidence not effort, a compute ledger per task.

**Statement carried by every report of this work: H-B3 (27 of 57 on Mealie, 47.4 percent, exact 95 percent interval 34.0 to 61.0, the 50 percent threshold NOT MET) is spent and is not re-adjudicated here. The detector measured here is v3 exactly as frozen at `0323e0b`; nothing in it changes. This is one further codebase and about twenty seeds: whatever it shows is preliminary, and the registered comparability rule is a decision convention, not a statistical test.**

## 1. Source

Owner decisions of 2026-11-03 (second message): (1) v4 not approved, not started. (2) Next milestone, the generalization test of frozen v3 (`0323e0b`, unchanged) on a second FastAPI codebase: (a) selection criteria pre-registered before browsing, then the pick and its reasons recorded; (b) the setup cost estimated and reported before building, and if the estimate exceeds about two days of agent work, stop and report options; (c) noise gate: comment-only controls across layers, zero unexplained differences, the same census procedure; (d) a new seed set by an independent agent under a new key (the owner keeps a copy), about twenty unintended across the ten classes (at least two each) and four intended (every seed shown), the same integrity analysis of the agent's log, and the new sentence in the brief "delete nothing outside your own working directory"; (e) the prediction and the comparability rule pre-registered; (f) no detector change of any kind: a defect the new app exposes is recorded, not fixed. (3) Positioning correction (done, register row 186). (4) Round 1-lite not launched, no paid run. (5) Standing rules.

## 2. Hypothesis and decision rule

| Row | Hypothesis | Measure | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| H-G1 | **Primary.** The frozen v3 detects, on a second FastAPI codebase, a share of unintended, test-passing, agent-written behaviour changes comparable to its Mealie share | r = unintended seeds with at least one unexplained difference / unintended seeds after pruning (the H-B3 definition), taken as the unrounded point estimate | Reference R = 27/57 = **47.4 percent**. **32.4 <= r <= 62.4: preliminary generalization.** **r < 32.4: recorded as a limit** (register row; v3's rate does not carry over to this app by the registered rule). **r > 62.4: recorded as above the band** (not lower than Mealie; no stronger claim than the band's, because a higher rate on one app may reflect an easier app or seed set). The band is closed at both ends |
| H-G1b | Reported beside it, not a gate | exact Clopper-Pearson 95 percent interval of r; Fisher's exact two-sided test of k/n against 27/57; whether 47.4 percent lies inside the interval; flagged counts per class | with about twenty seeds the interval half-width is about 20 points, wider than the band: neither "generalizes" nor "does not" is statistically established by this test, and every report says so |
| H-G1c | Intended seeds | the four outcomes of H-B3 section 5 | reported, no pass or fail; only "accepted and observed" counts as acceptance; false positives are reported with the number of observed intended seeds as their denominator |
| H-G1d | Noise | zero unexplained differences in each comment-only control (section 5) | a **condition of the freeze**, not a hypothesis: no freeze, no seed, until it holds |

For a given n the band is fixed in advance by arithmetic, so that no count is argued afterwards: at n = 20 it is 7 to 12 flagged (35.0 to 60.0 percent; 6 is below, 13 is above); at n = 22 it is 8 to 13; at n = 24 it is 8 to 14 (33.3 to 58.3 percent; 15 is 62.5 and above). `report.js` (not frozen, computes no diff result) prints the band for the n it is given.

v2 is **not** run on the new app (the owner asked for v3; a v2 arm would double the machine time and answers a different question). It can be added later as a separate, registered comparison.

## 3. Prediction, stated before any candidate is browsed and before any app-specific code

Reasoning. v3's Mealie result benefits from three rounds of learning on Mealie (v1, v2, v3 setups); v3's generator changes and its Mealie dataset were informed by the H-B2 misses. On a new app the engine and the generator carry over unchanged, but the setup (dataset, roles, ids) is a first pass written without that learning, so I expect the result to sit between v2's Mealie rate (22 to 26 percent) and v3's (47 percent), and I expect more of the misses to be configuration misses than on Mealie (6 of 30). A medium application with fewer operations and a simpler data model may be easier, which pushes the other way. With about twenty seeds the binomial noise alone is about 11 points.

- **H-G1 (rate):** I predict r in **20 to 50 percent, point estimate 35 percent.** My probabilities: r within the band (32.4 to 62.4) about **one in two**; r below 32.4 about **45 percent**; r above 62.4 about **5 percent**.
- **Noise gate:** the first full round of controls will **not** be clean (I put the probability that it is clean at 30 percent): a new app has nondeterminism I have not met (ids, timestamps in unusual formats, tokens, ordering), which the census will find.
- **Misses:** capability misses stay above half of all misses; the configuration share is **25 to 45 percent** (Mealie: 20 percent).
- **Intended seeds (4):** two or three accepted and observed; the probability of at least one false positive among the observed ones is about 25 percent.
- **A v3 defect:** the probability that the new app exposes at least one engine defect or limit not yet on the register (rows 145 to 150, 171 to 177, 182, 185) is about 60 percent. It will be registered, not fixed.
- **Setup cost:** reported in the cost estimate (section 9), not predicted here.

If the data contradict a prediction they are reported as they are.

## 4. Selection of the codebase (a), fixed before browsing

### 4.1 Gates, in this order, each evaluated at the pinned commit (the default-branch head at the time the candidate is examined; the commit is recorded)

Cheap gates (metadata and a shallow clone; no dependency is installed):

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| M1 | public, not archived, not a fork, not a mirror, an OSI-approved licence file present |
| M2 | **active:** last commit on the default branch on or after **2026-05-07** (180 days before today, 2026-11-03); at least 3 distinct commit authors since 2025-11-03; at least 300 commits in the history |
| M3 | **an application, not scaffolding:** it serves a domain through a FastAPI app with at least one persisted entity of its own. Rejected on the repository's own words: its description or README title calls it a template, starter, boilerplate, cookiecutter, tutorial, course, example, demo, awesome list, library, framework or extension. The rejecting sentence is recorded |
| M4 | **not previously used by ECCode and not ECCode's own:** not Mealie, not one of youtube-dl, black, tornado, thefuck, tqdm, not under `TOUMO45`, not a repository whose code is in `bench/suites/` or `tests/fixtures/` |
| M5 | **medium size:** between **10,000 and 60,000** physical lines of Python outside tests, migrations, docs, examples and scripts, counted by `git ls-files '*.py' \| grep -v -E '(^\|/)(tests?\|migrations?\|alembic\|docs?\|examples?\|scripts)/\|(^\|/)(test_[^/]*\|conftest)\.py$' \| xargs cat \| wc -l` |

Execution gates (dependencies installed in a fresh virtual environment under `~/.cache/eccode/`, no network at test and run time):

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| E1 | **installs here:** the project's lockfile or pinned requirements install from PyPI through the proxy with this container's interpreter (Python 3.13) or one `uv python install` can fetch here, in at most 20 minutes and 4 GB; no system package, GPU, container, or external service (database server, cache, queue, object store) is needed to run the app or its tests |
| E2 | **green suite:** the project's own CI test command (read from its CI workflow or README), unchanged except for environment variables that point storage at local files, passes **twice in a row** with **0 failed and 0 errors**, in at most **6 minutes** each; at least **100** tests pass; at least half of the test files send requests to the app through a test client. No test is deselected by me; only the project's own markers (as its CI uses them) may select |
| E3 | **OpenAPI exposed:** the app object imports offline; `app.openapi()` returns a valid document without authentication; the standard OpenAPI route is not disabled; the document has **40 to 400** operations, at least **15** with a JSON request body, and at least **5** distinct tags or router prefixes |
| E4 | **drivable offline:** `fastapi.testclient.TestClient(app)` answers `GET /openapi.json` with 200 under a closed network (sockets outside loopback raise), and at least one GET operation without path parameters answers below 500 |

### 4.2 Procedure

1. The candidate list is the result of one query, run once and stored whole in the selection record: `gh api -X GET search/repositories -f q='topic:fastapi language:python archived:false fork:false stars:>=200 pushed:>=2026-05-07' -f sort=stars -f order=desc -f per_page=40`.
2. Candidates are examined **in the order returned**. For each, gates M1 to M5 are evaluated; if all pass, E1 to E4. The **first candidate that passes every gate is selected** and examination stops. Rank is the only tie-breaker; I have no discretion to skip, re-order or prefer.
3. A gate failure is recorded with its measured value. Examination is lazy: candidates after the selected one are listed by name and rank only.
4. At most the 40 returned candidates are examined. If none passes, I stop and report the table, not relax a gate. Only an owner decision can skip a selected candidate, change a gate or extend the list.
5. The record is `bench/pilot-results/2026-11-03-hg1-selection.json` (query, time, full result list, per-candidate gate values, pinned commit) with the reasons in prose in the report.

### 4.3 What selection does not consider (anti-gaming)

Nothing about the detector: not the id format (uuid, integer, slug), the authentication scheme, the ORM, sync or async routes, the test style, the number of tests above the minimum, or whether v3's generator would fit the app. **No candidate is run through the behavior diff, the probe generator or any ECCode detector before the selection is recorded, and no seed is written or imagined for any candidate.** A codebase that v3 handles badly is as valid as one it handles well; what the setup then costs is measured in section 9 and reported.

## 5. Noise gate (c): condition of the freeze

As H-B3 step 3 (`bench/pilot/hb2/edit-check.js`), on the new app with the frozen engine:

- **Twelve comment-only controls: six files, two rounds.** One comment line is prepended to each file; every line number shifts. Files by a rule fixed here, from the app's import graph and operation table, chosen before any control runs: (1) the module whose dependents reach the most operations (the widest, by the static impact set); (2) the route module with the most operations; (3) the most-imported service or business-logic module; (4) the most-imported schema or model module; (5) the most-imported repository or data-access module; (6) the smallest route module in a different area from (2) with at least 3 operations. If a layer does not exist, the next most-imported module overall takes its place and the substitution is recorded.
- **Census:** six baseline runs (`behavior.baselineRuns` = 6) on the widest control. `ignoreFields` and `normalizers` grow **only** for fields the census or a control's differences show to be random per run, each with its census line and cause. Nothing else is added to the configuration for the noise gate.
- **The condition:** **zero unexplained differences in each of the twelve controls**, in the final configuration. A control that shows any is a failed round: the cause is found, the fix is made in the app's configuration or setup (never in the engine), and **all twelve are repeated**. Every failed round and its cause is reported, not only the passing one.
- **Wall-time bound:** the widest control must finish in at most 15 minutes (the H-B3 bound). If it does not, the number of `exampleIds` variants is reduced before the freeze.
- If a noise source cannot be removed by configuration (it needs an engine change), I stop and report: no engine change (section 8).

## 6. The seed set (d)

- **A new independent agent** in a fresh context, given only the committed brief (H-B3's, adapted: section 6.1) and nothing about the detector, v2, v3, the H-B2 or H-B3 sets or results. **A new 32-byte key**, generated by a command that prints nothing, stored outside the repository with mode 600, never read, printed or logged by the implementer; **the owner receives a copy as a file attachment, not as text**.
- **About twenty unintended seeds:** the sealing tool requires **at least 20 and at least 2 in each of the ten classes** (status-code, message-text, default-value, boundary, permission, field-exposure, validation, ordering, side-effect, format). **Four intended seeds** (contract declared before the diff).
- **Every seed shown** (the H-B3 amendment 1 rule, kept): `verification.shown: true` with evidence of at least 40 characters saying the request was exercised on the unpatched and the patched application and how the responses differ; a seed the agent cannot show is not sealed.
- **Spread:** at least 40 percent of the unintended seeds change code below the route layer; at least 4 different areas of the app (routers or domains); each seed one to three files, at most 30 changed lines, applies alone, edits no test, depends on no clock, randomness or environment variable.
- **Integrity analysis of the agent's whole tool log** by the H-B3 analysis (`audit-agent-log.js` logic, re-created for the new paths as a new file with its test): every flagged item listed and adjudicated, none exempted afterwards; if the agent read any detector, probe, configuration or test file of ECCode, H-G1 is marked **non-independent** in every table.
- **Validation** by the registered rule: the app's whole test suite per seed, alone, in restart-tolerant chunks. **Minimum after pruning: 16 unintended seeds and 2 in each class**, else stop and report before measuring (a replacement set would be a new agent and a new registration).

### 6.1 Changes to the H-B3 brief (the whole list; everything else is the H-B3 text with the Mealie names replaced by the app's)

1. The new sentence: **"Delete nothing outside your own working directory."** (At H-B3 the agent deleted the session's background task output files after sealing; reported as a deviation, register row 183.)
2. Counts: at least 20 unintended and at least 2 per class; 4 intended (not 6 to 10); at least 4 areas (not 5).
3. The application, its commit, checkout path, test command and fixtures; the OpenAPI examples are the app's.
4. The sealing command is the new tool (`bench/pilot/hg1/seal.js`), the same checks with the new minimums.

The brief is committed with its sha256 before launch, and says nothing about the measuring tool.

## 7. Measurement

v3 (primary, only) from a git worktree at the **new freeze commit** (section 8), one engine run per seed, no agent, no paid model run, frozen-file hashes checked before and after by the driver and by independent recomputation. **The results are hashed and committed before any seed description, file name, `observableBy` or patch is read.** Only then are the misses classified (capability or configuration, by the H-B rule: configuration only if every blocker could be removed by what `behavior.json` can supply) and the detections checked for attribution. A second blind labeller on a seeded sample of the misses (at most 6) is planned, as in H-B2 and H-B3, and is reported as a post-measurement judgment with agreement and Cohen's kappa; the owner can strike it. No re-run with a changed configuration is made to test a label.

## 8. Freeze and "no detector change" (f)

- **Unchanged and verified by hash:** the 40 files of the v3 manifest (`bench/pilot/phase2-freeze-v3.json`) under `bin/`, `lib/` and `scripts/behavior/` (the engine closure, including `lib/behavior.js` and the probe runner with the example generator). A check compares their hashes with the manifest at the freeze and again before and after measurement; any difference voids the run.
- **The eight bench-tooling files of the manifest are Mealie-coupled** (`mealieCommit`, the Mealie checkout, the sealed-set path, the Mealie configuration and setup). They are **not edited**: for the new app they are re-created as new files under `bench/pilot/hg1/` (configuration generator, setup/population script, per-seed runner, seed harness, sealing tool, seed validator, log analysis), each derived from its H-B3 counterpart, with the derivation recorded and tested.
- **What the per-app work may do** is exactly what `docs/behavior-diff.md` offers as configuration: `app`, `env`, `setup`, `roles`, `exampleIds`, `normalizers`, `ignoreFields`, `unordered`, `baselineRuns`. Nothing else.
- **A new freeze file** (`bench/pilot/phase2-freeze-hg1.json`) lists the 40 engine files with their v3 hashes and the new app files with theirs, committed and pushed **before any key or seed exists**; the freeze commit hash is recorded here before the key is generated.
- **After the freeze nothing changes** (pipeline, configuration, brief, seeds). A change is a new version, a new freeze and a new registration; what was measured before it is exploratory. The analysis code is not frozen and computes no diff result.
- **A v3 defect the app exposes** (a crash, a wrong normalization, an unreachable surface, a nondeterminism the configuration cannot remove) is **recorded on the limits register with its evidence and is not fixed.** If it prevents a valid measurement, I stop and report with options.

## 9. Setup-cost estimate (b)

Before anything is built on the selected app, `docs/design/hg1-setup-cost.md` reports, from measured evidence on the app (install time, suite time, operation count, authentication and roles, entity kinds, a probe-corpus size and a baseline-run time on the unmodified app, the Mealie costs as the reference): the work for (1) `behavior.json`, (2) the population step with deterministic ids, (3) the noise controls and census, (4) the re-created harness, (5) the seeding, validation and measurement runs, (6) the report. It gives agent-work time and machine wall time separately. **If the estimate exceeds about two days of agent work, I stop and report options** (a smaller scope, the next candidate by the rule if the owner decides, or a different split) and build nothing.

## 10. Limits registered with this protocol

One codebase; about twenty unintended seeds (the interval is wide); the seeds are written by one agent, not found in the wild; v3's setup and generator carry Mealie lessons while the new app's setup is a first pass; the setup is written by the same agent that knows what v3 can observe, which is a risk to comparability that the registered selection procedure and freeze do not remove; the independence of the seeding agent rests on its brief and its log analysis; the query reaches only repositories tagged `fastapi`; the comparability rule is a convention for describing the result; this measures the freeze commit and says nothing about later engine changes; agent compute is in the ledger and is not metered by the benchmark meter, which stays at $0; Round 1-lite is not launched.
