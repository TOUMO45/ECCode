# Groundwork: product-owner sign-off package

**Why this exists.** The acceptance report marks Groundwork as delivered but "a pass that a product owner has not signed off": every action the engine reserves for `--actor user` was entered by an operator or, on 2026-10-09, by the orchestrator on the user's instruction. The record is complete; what is missing is a person's decision on the items below. This page lists each decision, what accepting it means, and the exact command that records it, so sign-off is one reply.

**How to sign off.** Run the commands yourself in a terminal in `examples/groundwork` (since 0.3.0 `--actor user` is confirmed at the terminal: the CLI shows what it will record and waits for `yes`), or grant the orchestrator a bounded delegation per item (`eccode delegate grant --actor user --to orchestrator --action risk.accept --target RISK-1 --reason "<your words>"`, then the orchestrator runs the command with `--actor orchestrator --delegation <id>`). An orchestrator can no longer type these in as yours. Either way the handoff's "User decisions" section will list them. Nothing here is pre-accepted.

RISK-13 and RISK-15 are `mitigated` with current mitigation text but keep the titles of their original findings; closing them (`eccode risk update --id RISK-13 --status closed --actor orchestrator --mitigation "…"`) is bookkeeping the orchestrator can do on your word. After the commands: `eccode audit` (must stay OK) and `eccode status --brief`. Note that a future `eccode deliver` of Groundwork under 0.3.0 would be refused while RISK-1, RISK-2, RISK-10 and RISK-14 are open (release policy: critical and high risks need a disposition), which is the same decision this page asks for. No gate needs to be reopened; the decisions are recorded against the delivered build. If you reject an item, say which: a rejection of RISK-10, for example, is a product change (fallback-only shipping) that goes through `/eccode:change`.

**What this page does not do.** It does not accept anything on your behalf and it is not evidence of acceptance. Until the commands are run, the acceptance report's wording stands.
